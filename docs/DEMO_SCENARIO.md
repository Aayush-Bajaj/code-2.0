# Demo Scenario: Sharma Auto Components, Pune

## 1. Enterprise Profile
- **Company Name:** Sharma Auto Components Pvt. Ltd.
- **Facility Location:** Plot 42, Sector 10, Bhosari MIDC, Pune, Maharashtra 411026, India.
- **Industry & Activity:** Tier-2 Precision Auto Machining & Stamping (supplying engine brackets, transmission housings, and suspension components to Pune Tier-1 automotive exporters).
- **Facility Footprint:** 22,000 sq. ft covered workshop area, 4,000 sq. ft open yard.
- **Operating Shifts:** 2 shifts/day (16 hours total), 26 operational days per month.

## 2. On-Site Stationary & Mobile Equipment Inventory

### 2.1 Diesel Generator Set (Backup Power)
- **Equipment:** Kirloskar Green 125 kVA Silent DG Set (Engine: 6R1080TA).
- **Rated Power:** 100 kW / 125 kVA.
- **Operating Hours:** 45 hours / month (intermittent load shedding and peak shaving during grid maintenance).
- **Observed Condition:** Video evidence reveals heavy black exhaust smoke during load step changes (triggering optical smoke score 8.2 and maintenance flag for injector fouling).
- **Average Load:** 75% rated capacity.

### 2.2 Internal Logistics (Forklift)
- **Equipment:** Godrej 3.0 Ton Diesel Forklift (Model: GX 300 D).
- **Rated Power:** 44 kW diesel engine.
- **Operating Hours:** 120 hours / month (moving raw steel coils, forgings, and finished bins).
- **Fuel Consumption:** ~3.2 Litres / hour.

### 2.3 Outbound Commercial Delivery Truck
- **Equipment:** Tata 1613 LPT 16-Ton Multi-Axle Rigid Truck.
- **Operating Scope:** Factory-to-port and Tier-1 hub freight runs (Pune to JNPT Nhava Sheva & Chakan/Sanand corridors).
- **Monthly Distance / Activity:** 3,200 km / month carrying an average 9.5 tonnes (approx. 30,400 tonne-km / month).
